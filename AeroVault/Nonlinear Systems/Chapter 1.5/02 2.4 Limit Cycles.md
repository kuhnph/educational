
- **oscillation** occurs for *nontrivial periodic solutions*
	$$x(t+T)=x(t)$$
- **Harmonic** oscillators have an infinite repeating pattern (ideal spring)

## LC Circuit Example

LC circuit satisfies:
$$L\ddot{q}+\frac{1}{C}q=0$$
Change of state:
$$z_1=q=charge$$
$$z_2=\dot{q}=current$$
Write the equation:
$$\ddot{z}+\beta z=0$$
Where:
$$\beta=\frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}$$
