### Break the pant into four groups

$$
\boxed{ \begin{aligned} \dot{\mathbf{p}} &= \mathbf{f}_{pos}(\mathbf{x})\\ \dot{\mathbf{V}}_b &= \mathbf{f}_{trans}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{u})\\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\eta}} &= \mathbf{f}_{kin}(\mathbf{x})\\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}} &= \mathbf{f}_{rot}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{u}) \end{aligned} }
$$
$$
\mathbf{V}_b= \begin{bmatrix} u\\v\\w \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \boldsymbol{\eta}= \begin{bmatrix} \phi\\\theta\\\psi \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \boldsymbol{\omega}= \begin{bmatrix} p\\q\\r \end{bmatrix}.
$$
---
# Translational Plant

Body frame translational equation:
$$
\boxed{m(\mathbf{\dot{V}_b+\omega\times\mathbf{v_b}})=\mathbf{F}_b}
$$
Or:
$$
\boxed{ \dot{\mathbf V}_b = \frac{1}{m}\mathbf F_b - \boldsymbol{\omega}\times\mathbf V_b }
$$

And:
$$
\mathbf{V}_b=
\begin{bmatrix}
\dot{\mathbf{u}}\\
\dot{\mathbf{v}}\\
\dot{\mathbf{w}}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
Where:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
\dot u = rv-qw+\frac{F_x}{m} \\
\dot v = pw-ru+\frac{F_y}{m} \\ 
\dot w = qu-pv+\frac{F_z}{m}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
---
# Rigid Body Rotational Plant

$$
\boxed{ \mathbf J\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}} + \boldsymbol{\omega} \times \left( \mathbf J\boldsymbol{\omega} \right) = \mathbf M }
$$

Where:
$$
\boldsymbol{\omega} = \begin{bmatrix} p\\q\\r \end{bmatrix}
$$

And:
$$\mathbf M = \begin{bmatrix} L\\M\\N \end{bmatrix}$$

- L = roll moment
- M = Pitch Moment
- N = Yaw moment

Aircraft inertia matrix symmetric about the $x$-$z$ plane:
$$
\mathbf J= \begin{bmatrix} J_x & 0 & -J_{xz}\\ 0 & J_y & 0\\ -J_{xz} & 0 & J_z \end{bmatrix}
$$

Now get the invertible plant model:

$$
\boxed{ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}} = \mathbf J^{-1} \left[ \mathbf M - \boldsymbol{\omega} \times \left( \mathbf J\boldsymbol{\omega} \right) \right] }
$$

Component wise
$$
\begin{bmatrix} \dot p\\ \dot q\\ \dot r \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf J^{-1} \left[ \begin{bmatrix} L\\M\\N \end{bmatrix} - \boldsymbol{\omega} \times \left( \mathbf J\boldsymbol{\omega} \right) \right]
$$
---


# Aerodynamic Moment Model

Plant:
$$
\boxed{M=M(\mathbf{x,u})}
$$
Component wise:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} L\\M\\N \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} L(\mathbf x,\delta_a,\delta_r)\\ M(\mathbf x,\delta_e)\\ N(\mathbf x,\delta_a,\delta_r) \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
L = q*Sb\,C_\ell \\
M = q*Sc\,C_m \\ 
N = q*Sb\,C_n
\end{bmatrix}
$$
*Where $q=\frac{1}{2}\rho*V_a^2$ 

Then moment coefficient:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
C_\ell = C_{\ell_0} + C_{\ell_\beta}\beta + C_{\ell_p}\frac{bp}{2V_a} + C_{\ell_r}\frac{br}{2V_a} + C_{\ell_{\delta_a}}\delta_a + C_{\ell_{\delta_r}}\delta_r \\
C_m = C_{m_0} + C_{m_\alpha}\alpha + C_{m_q}\frac{cq}{2V_a} + C_{m_{\delta_e}}\delta_e \\
C_n = C_{n_0} + C_{n_\beta}\beta + C_{n_p}\frac{bp}{2V_a} + C_{n_r}\frac{br}{2V_a} + C_{n_{\delta_a}}\delta_a + C_{n_{\delta_r}}\delta_r
\end{bmatrix}
$$

# Separating Natural Moments and Control Generated Moments

DI makes use of: $\boxed{M=M_0(x)+B_M(x)u_{rot}}$
$M_0$ contains moments generated by the aircraft:
- angle of attack
- sideslip
- angular rates
- aerodynamic bias

$\mathbf{B}_Mu_{rot}$ contains the moments generated by control surfaces:
$$
u_{rot} =
\begin{bmatrix}
\delta_a \\
\delta_e \\
\delta_r
\end{bmatrix}
$$
and the control effectiveness matrix has the form:
$$
\mathbf B_M = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial L}{\partial\delta_a} & \frac{\partial L}{\partial\delta_e} & \frac{\partial L}{\partial\delta_r} \\[6pt] \frac{\partial M}{\partial\delta_a} & \frac{\partial M}{\partial\delta_e} & \frac{\partial M}{\partial\delta_r} \\[6pt] \frac{\partial N}{\partial\delta_a} & \frac{\partial N}{\partial\delta_e} & \frac{\partial N}{\partial\delta_r} \end{bmatrix}
$$

Then reduced for a conventional aircraft to:
$$
\mathbf B_M \approx \begin{bmatrix} L_{\delta_a} & 0 & L_{\delta_r}\\ 0 & M_{\delta_e} & 0\\ N_{\delta_a} & 0 & N_{\delta_r} \end{bmatrix}
$$

Where there is a change of notation:
$$
L_{\delta_r} \equiv \frac{\partial L}{\partial \delta_r}, \qquad M_{\delta_e} \equiv \frac{\partial M}{\partial \delta_e}, N_{\delta_a} \equiv \frac{\partial N}{\partial \delta_a}, \qquad N_{\delta_r} \equiv \frac{\partial N}{\partial \delta_r}
$$
---

# Plant in Control-Affine Form

The moment decomposition replacing the internals of: $\boxed{ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}} = \mathbf J^{-1} \left[ \mathbf M - \boldsymbol{\omega} \times \left( \mathbf J\boldsymbol{\omega} \right) \right] }$  turns into:
$$
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}} = \mathbf J^{-1} \left[ \mathbf M_0(\mathbf x) + \mathbf B_M(\mathbf x)\mathbf u_{rot} - \boldsymbol{\omega} \times (\mathbf J\boldsymbol{\omega}) \right]
$$

Now the control-affine form can be written as:
$$
\boxed{ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}} = \mathbf f_\omega(\mathbf x) + \mathbf G_\omega(\mathbf x)\mathbf u_{rot} }
$$
where:
$$
\boxed{ \mathbf f_\omega(\mathbf x) = \mathbf J^{-1} \left[ \mathbf M_0(\mathbf x) - \boldsymbol{\omega} \times (\mathbf J\boldsymbol{\omega}) \right] }
$$

And:
$$
\boxed{ \mathbf G_\omega(\mathbf x) = \mathbf J^{-1}\mathbf B_M(\mathbf x)}
$$

---
# Inversion

Now the inversion step can done by finding a $\nu=f(x)+G(x)u$ to equal some desired $\dot\omega$ so that $u$ can be solved for to obtain:
$$
u = (v-f(x))G^{-1}(x)
$$
